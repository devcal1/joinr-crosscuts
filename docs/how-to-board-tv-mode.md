# How to show the board on the factory TV (TV mode)

For the laptop connected to the TV in the factory.

1. Open **Scheduling > Board** on the laptop.
2. Press **TV mode** (next to PDF and JPG, at the top right of the week strip). The screen goes fullscreen and shows the current week, scaled up as large as it will go without scrolling. The menu bar, week buttons and the edit controls are hidden, and clicking does nothing, so nobody changes the board by accident.
3. To leave, press **Esc**, or move the mouse and press **Exit TV mode** at the top right. The cursor and that button hide a few seconds after the mouse stops.

While TV mode is on, the screen is kept awake, the data reloads every 5 minutes, and on Monday morning (when the date changes) it moves on to the new week by itself.

Tips:

- Set the laptop not to sleep when plugged in (Windows: Settings, System, Power, Screen and sleep), and keep the lid open or set "closing the lid" to do nothing. TV mode asks the browser to keep the screen on, but not every browser allows it.
- Browsers only go fullscreen after a click, so TV mode cannot start by itself when the page loads. Someone has to press the button after the laptop has been switched on.
- If the laptop is restarted or the browser is closed, open the Board and press **TV mode** again.
- Print and the JPG export are not affected by TV mode.
