# How to set up recurring tasks

Admins only. Open **Admin**, then **Tasks**.

## Add one

1. Click **Add recurring task**.
2. Type the **Title** and, if you like, a **Note**.
3. Under **For**, pick **one person** or **one department** (Scheduling, Projects, Ordering, Accounts, Admin).
   - A person gets the task in their own Tasks list.
   - A department gets **one shared task**: everyone in it sees it, and whoever ticks it closes it for everyone.
4. Pick how it **Repeats**: every week, fortnight, month or year, or **Custom** (every N days, weeks, months or years).
5. Set the **First due date** and, if it should stop, an **End date**.
6. **Show it this many days before it is due** (0 to 60): the task appears that many days early. 0 means it appears on the day it is due.
7. **Save.** Anything already due is made straight away.

Each new task sends an alert (to the person, or to everyone in the department). Each one also shows
on the **Planner** on its due date: a person's on their Personal layer, a department's on its layer.

## Good to know

- A monthly or yearly task due on the 29th, 30th or 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month.
- A task not ticked by its due date stays open and shows **overdue** in red. The next one still comes on time.
- Nothing is made for dates before you added the rule.
- **Pause** stops new tasks; **Resume** starts again from today (paused days are skipped).
- **Delete** stops new tasks. Tasks it has already made stay where they are.
- **Edit** changes what comes next; tasks already made keep their own title and date.
