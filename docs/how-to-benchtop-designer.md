# How to draw a benchtop plan

Each job has one benchtop plan: the stone pieces, drawn to scale, ready to print for the stone supplier.

## Set up the lists (admins, once)

Admin > **Benchtop lists**. Add each material (supplier, range, colour, thickness in mm) and check the edge finishes (code, name, line style). Untick **Shown** to hide one from new choices; plans that already use it keep it.

## Start a plan

Open the job, go to its **Benchtops** section and click **Benchtop plan**. Existing plans are also listed on Projects > Benchtops.

## Add pieces

Click **Straight**, **L**, **U** or **Waterfall leg**. For each piece:

- Type each run's length and depth in mm (depth starts at 600). Lengths are full outside lengths.
- Pick the material: supplier and range, colour, thickness.
- A waterfall leg has a width and height and is mitred to an end of another piece (choose which piece and which end).

## Edges, joins and cut-outs

- **Edges:** click an edge in the drawing (Select), then choose its finish on the right. Finishes are shown by line style and name; teal only marks what is selected.
- **Joins:** click **Place join**, then click along a run. Adjust the position (mm from the run's start) on the right.
- **Cut-outs:** click **Add cut-out**. Choose the kind (sink, cooktop, tap hole, other), size, the edge it is measured from, the distance along that edge and the distance in from it (both to the cut-out's near sides).

Type notes for the supplier on the left, then **Save**.

## Print

**Print plan set** saves, then opens the plan set. If anything changed since the last revision, it becomes the next letter (A, B, C...). Use the browser's print to save a PDF (A4 portrait). Older revisions are listed on the left: **Open** shows one read-only, **Print** reprints it.
