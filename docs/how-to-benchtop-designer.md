# How to draw a benchtop plan

Each job has one benchtop plan: the stone pieces, drawn to scale, ready to print for the stone supplier.

## Set up the lists (admins, once)

Admin > **Benchtop lists**. Add each material (supplier, range, colour, thickness in mm) and check the edge finishes (code, name, line style). Untick **Shown** to hide one from new choices; plans that already use it keep it.

## Start a plan

Open the job, go to its **Benchtops** section and click **Benchtop plan**. Existing plans are also listed on Projects > Benchtops.

## Add pieces

Click **Straight**, **L shape (left)**, **L shape (right)** or **U**. The plan opens on **All**, which draws every piece together to scale. Drag a piece to arrange it (for example the island in front of the run); the arrangement is saved and printed. Click a piece in the list to select it; click it again to see it on its own, or **All** to go back. The red × on the selected piece deletes it (you are asked first).

- **Sizes:** click any size on the drawing, type the new length in mm and press Enter (Esc cancels). Depths are the end edges' sizes, typed the same way. New runs start 600 deep.
- **Material:** on the right, pick supplier and range, colour, thickness.

## Edges, joins and cut-outs

- **Edges:** click an edge, then choose its finish on the right. Finishes are shown by line style and name; teal only marks what is selected.
- **Waterfall:** on an end edge, choose **Waterfall** and type the drop height (mm). It shows as "WF 870" on screen and on the print.
- **Joins:** click **Place join**, then click along a run. Adjust the position (mm from the run's start) on the right.
- **Cut-outs:** select a piece and click **Add cut-out**. Choose the kind (sink, cooktop, tap hole, other), size, the edge it is measured from, the distance along that edge and the distance in from it (both to the cut-out's near sides).

Type notes for the supplier on the left, then **Save**.

## Print

**Print plan set** saves, then opens the plan set: the All arrangement to scale on the first sheet (pieces go across sheets only if it does not fit), the legend, cut-outs, material schedule and notes. If anything changed since the last revision, it becomes the next letter (A, B, C...). Use the browser's print to save a PDF (A4 portrait). Older revisions are listed on the left: **Open** shows one read-only, **Print** reprints it.
