How it works. A job card is the job's order lines (the same items show on the job's Orders & materials tab), now with a quantity. Cards show while anything on them isn't received; tick Show finished to see the rest. Supplier filters the cards to jobs with items from that supplier. The old line-by-line list stays on the All order lines tab, unchanged. Receiving still happens on Deliveries. Add items: anyone who can edit orders. Ticking items ordered (the supplier order): Mark ordered holders, as now.
