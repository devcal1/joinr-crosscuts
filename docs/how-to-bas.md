# How to use BAS sales in Crosscuts

For Todd and the accounts team. **BAS sales** keeps each month's sales for the BAS, which you **type in by hand** from your own records (MYOB or the bank). Invoices in Crosscuts are not used: nothing is worked out for you.

Anyone with Accounts access can see BAS sales. Typing in sales and lodging need the Accounts edit access (the same as invoicing). Only an admin can reopen a lodged month.

Open **Accounts**, then **BAS sales**.

## The figures

- **Sales inc GST (G1)** is the month's total sales including GST, as you type it.
- **GST (1A)** is the GST in that figure, as you type it. It is optional: leave it blank if you don't know it.
- **Ex GST** is the difference (blank if no GST was typed).

GST can't be more than a tenth of the sales before GST, and it has the same sign as the sales. A month with no sales at all is a real entry of 0. A negative amount is fine (net refunds), with a note.

## The year table

The page opens on the current financial year (July to June, named by the year it ends: FY 2027 is Jul 2026 to Jun 2027). **Previous year** and **Next year** move between years, back to FY 2023.

Every month up to the current one has a row. It stays **blank, with Not entered**, until you type its sales, and each new month appears by itself. The row shows its figures and a status:
- **Lodged** with the date, once it's lodged;
- **Not ended** for the current month (once typed in);
- **Not entered** for a month with nothing typed in.

A dash in GST or Ex GST means the sales were typed without GST. The quarter rows and the year total add up the months that are entered, and show a dash too if any of those has no GST. Months with nothing entered add nothing.

**Download CSV** gives the year as a spreadsheet: one line per month, with Sales inc GST, GST, Ex GST and the note. **Print** prints the year table with its quarter and year totals (choose Save as PDF to keep a copy).

## Typing in a month

Press a month to open it below the table, and fill in **This month's sales**: the amount including GST, its GST (optional) and a note. Press **Save**. **Clear** removes what was typed, so the month is blank again. **Previous month** and **Next month** step through months, so past months can be filled in one after another, from June 2023.

Use the note for where the figure came from (for example "From the old workbook").

## Lodging

When the BAS for a month is done, open the month and press **Lodge**. Crosscuts asks you to confirm, naming the month and its Sales inc GST. A month can be lodged only once it has ended, and only if its sales have been typed in. For a quarterly BAS, lodge each of its three months; the quarter row shows how many are lodged.

A lodged month's sales can't be changed. To correct one, an admin opens it and presses **Reopen**; it can then be changed and lodged again.
