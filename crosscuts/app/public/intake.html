New from Joinr

A quote won in Joinr becomes a job here by itself, and the Design/PM team gets a task and an alert. If exactly one job already has the quote number, the quote is linked to that job instead. If two or more jobs have it, nothing is made: the quote waits here for you to choose which job to Link. This page is for the exceptions: a quote the system could not handle waits under “Waiting for a person” for you to Accept, Link or Ignore, and the other tabs are the history.
